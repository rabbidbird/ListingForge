# SellerDrafts $300 paid-social test

Status: prepared, not launched. Do not add a payment method or activate ads until the operator approves the final campaign in the advertising account.

## Platform decision

Start with TikTok. Its vertical format and seller/side-hustle audience fit the product better than X for this first creative test. TikTok does not publish a guaranteed U.S. new-advertiser credit; check **Ads Manager → Billing → Promotions** before launch and treat only a promotion shown in the account as available.

X currently advertises a $500 credit after an eligible new advertiser spends $500. That offer does not fit this $300 test, so do not increase the test budget merely to chase the credit.

## Campaign setup

- Objective: website traffic initially; optimize toward a server-recorded signup only after enough conversion data exists.
- Destination: `https://sellerdrafts.com/`
- Geography: United States.
- Audience: adults interested in Etsy selling, handmade businesses, ecommerce, product photography, craft businesses, and small-business tools. Keep targeting broad enough to learn; do not target sensitive attributes.
- Campaign budget: no limit at campaign level.
- One ad group with a $300 lifetime budget over 12 days ($25/day average, above TikTok's current $20/day ad-group minimum).
- Rotate all three creatives evenly at launch.
- Check at 72 hours and at $150 spent. Pause only for a broken destination, policy issue, or clearly unusable traffic. Select the winner at the midpoint using verified signup rate first and click-through rate second.
- Hard stop: $300 total paid spend. A promotional credit may extend delivery only if it is already active in the account and its terms are understood.

## Creative copy

### Facts to draft

Primary text: Etsy listing drafts that stay inside the facts. Add the product details you know, create a structured draft, then verify it before publishing.

CTA: Sign Up

### Three-step flow

Primary text: Add your product facts. Create a structured Etsy draft. Find it later in History. SellerDrafts keeps the path short and the draft clearly marked for review.

CTA: Sign Up

### Nothing extra

Primary text: Your facts. Nothing extra. SellerDrafts does not add unsupported materials, shipping promises, bestseller language, or other claims you did not supply.

CTA: Sign Up

## Measurement

Use the application's attribution report as the source of truth for UTM visits, signups, checkout starts, and completed checkouts. Do not call a campaign profitable from clicks alone. With a $12 Starter price, the first test is for message and signup signal; a statistically reliable paid-acquisition conclusion will require more than a $300 sample.
